How to use:
1. Pan and zoom the map to frame the area you want to check
2. Choose your date range (real imagery available from 1984, subject to actual scene/cloud-cover availability)
3. Click "Run Analysis" to scan the entire visible map area

The whole visible map is analyzed, not just a point -- zoom in for a smaller, more detailed area, or out for wider coverage (up to about 2° across). A dashed outline shows exactly what gets analyzed. Only real satellite imagery is used; if no cloud-free scene exists near a chosen date, the analysis will report that instead of guessing.
